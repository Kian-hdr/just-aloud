JUST ALOUD

Drag Just Aloud.app onto the Applications folder beside it.
Then eject this disk image and open Just Aloud from Applications.

Just Aloud lives in the menu bar. Click its waveform to open playback controls
and Settings. Welcome & Setup explains ElevenLabs and Accessibility access.

No Homebrew or installer script is required for this download.
An existing Speak11 installation is not changed.

To uninstall, quit Just Aloud and move it from Applications to Trash.
Your settings and Keychain credential are preserved.
