## Source availability

Editable icon sources live in [`Design/Icon`](Design/Icon). `JustAloud.icon` is
the Apple Icon Composer document. The layer SVGs are 1024 × 1024 transparent
vector sources. The `.icns` and asset catalog provide compatibility fallbacks.

System-provided macOS masks, shadows, highlights, and materials must remain
system-generated. Do not add a rounded-square plate or baked outer shadow to
the vector artwork.
