# Just Aloud icon brief

The mark combines an open listening arc, a central play shape, and two outgoing
sound waves. Its silhouette remains legible at 16 px and does not depend on
text, initials, an outer app-icon plate, or another company’s branding.

Color roles:

- Indigo: listening and focus
- Aqua: audible output
- Coral: playback action

Icon Composer should apply platform materials, lighting, masking, highlights,
and shadows. The source vectors do not bake those effects into the artwork.
